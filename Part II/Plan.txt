Room Renovation Plan

Goal:
- Transform boring room into a cozy space within 72h.

Materials:
- Masking tape,
- Foil,
- White paint,
- Wooden rack,
- Decor items.


Steps:
	Day 1 - Prep (Estimated: 2h)
1. Move small furniture to the corridor.
2. Cover large furniture with foil.
3. Tape over sockets and switches.

	Day 2 - Main Loop (Estimated: 5h)
4. Paint the walls.
5. Clean unprotected areas.
6. Wash brushes, take out empty paint cans.
7. While drying, assemble the new rack in the corridor.
Risk: Paints dries too slowly. Mitigation: Maximize room ventilation.

	Day 3 - Polish (Estimated: 3h)
8. Remove all tape and foil.
9. Clean up any remaining stains.
10. Bring in the rack.
11. Place flowers on the rack.
12. Arrange decor, hang curtains.
Check: Verify rack stability.
